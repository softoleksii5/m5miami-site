What a condo, kitchen, bathroom or full renovation costs in Miami

No two renovations are the same, so we do not quote a price per square foot. This page explains what moves the number, and the one figure we stand behind: a typical full renovation project with M5 is $200–250K.

How to read this

How to read these numbers

A price per square foot hides almost everything that matters: the building, the layout, the finish level and the condition of what is behind the walls. We price every project after visiting the space, so there is no guessing over the phone. You get an itemized estimate 48 hours after the visit.

Full renovation

Full condo and full house renovation

A typical full renovation project with M5 is $200–250K. What moves a project within and beyond that figure:

  • Size and scope. How many rooms, and how much changes: finishes only, or layouts, kitchens and bathrooms too.
  • The building. In a condo, building rules, approvals and work hours shape the schedule. In a house, structure, roof and openings matter more. See condo and apartment renovation and full home renovation.
  • Finish level. Stone, joinery, plaster and fixtures vary widely in price.
  • Condition. Wiring, plumbing and moisture behind the walls can change the plan once opened.
Kitchens

Kitchens

The cost of a kitchen follows how much of it moves. A new layout with relocated plumbing and ventilation costs more than the same footprint with new cabinets. Other drivers are the cabinetry and joinery, stone counters and islands, appliances and lighting. Read about condo kitchens and house kitchens.

Bathrooms

Bathrooms

Bathrooms are small rooms with a lot of detail: layout, waterproofing, stone or tile, fixtures and lighting. Plaster is an option too, since Venetian plaster and marmorino work in wet areas with a protective sealer. See condo bathrooms and house bathrooms.

Design fees

Design fees

Design can be bought on its own or as part of a full renovation. The fee depends on the size of the home and the scope, and we state it in writing after the site visit. See interior design with M5.

FAQ

Questions

How much does it cost to renovate a condo in Miami?

A typical full renovation project with M5 is $200–250K. The exact price depends on the size, the scope, the finish level and the building. We price every project after visiting the space.

What does a kitchen remodel cost in Miami?

It depends on how much of the kitchen moves, on the cabinetry and stone, and on the appliances. We give an itemized price after seeing the space.

What does a bathroom remodel cost in Miami?

It depends on the layout, the waterproofing, the stone or tile and the fixtures. We give an itemized price after seeing the space.

Why do renovation quotes differ so much?

Often the scope is not the same: one quote leaves out work another includes, or uses allowances instead of real prices. An itemized budget that changes only through a change order you sign first makes quotes comparable.
