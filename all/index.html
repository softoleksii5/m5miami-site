FAQ

Questions we hear first

Do you subcontract the work?

Design, project management and supervision are in-house. Construction is performed by licensed & insured Florida contractor partners under their own permit and contract. One team answers for the result.

Who handles permits and the condo board?

We coordinate it. Miami-Dade permits are pulled by our licensed contractor partners; COI for your building, elevator bookings and noise-hour rules are part of our process — not your problem.

How much does a project cost?

We price every project after visiting the space — no guessing over the phone. You get an itemized budget at current Miami market rates before you sign, and it changes only through a change order you approve first. Venetian plaster projects start from $10,000.

How long does it take?

A typical renovation takes up to 3 months. You get the timeline upfront and weekly photo updates all the way through.

Is there a warranty?

Yes: a 1-year written warranty. The same team that delivered your project fixes any issue after handover.
