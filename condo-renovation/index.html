Kitchens

Kitchens in a condo

A condo kitchen is shaped by the building before it is shaped by the design: where plumbing and ventilation can run, which walls are shared, and what the association allows. We check what can move first, then design around it.

  • Layout. Including opening a closed kitchen to the living room where the building allows it.
  • Joinery and stone. Cabinetry, counters and islands, from light oak to black stone.
  • Lighting and appliances. Planned together with ventilation, so nothing is added as an afterthought.

Building rules

Building rules, board approval and work hours

Every building has its own rules. Typically that means association approval for the plans, restricted work hours, elevator reservations and an insurance certificate (COI) for the building. Always check your own building's rules. Ours is to take the paperwork off your hands.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need the association's approval to renovate my condo in Miami?

In most buildings, yes, for anything beyond cosmetic work. Every association has its own rules, so check your building's declaration and rules first. We prepare the paperwork and coordinate the building process; the exact requirements come from your association.

What can and cannot be changed in a condo renovation?

Inside your unit, finishes, kitchens, bathrooms and non-structural layouts can usually be redesigned. Structure, shared plumbing stacks and exterior elements are usually off limits or need special approval. Your association's rules and the building decide, so we check both before we design.

How long does a full condo renovation take?

It depends on the size, the scope and the building's approval process. The design stage takes roughly 4–6 weeks, from the first consultation to the design, 3D and itemized budget. The construction time follows the estimate, with weekly photo updates. You get the timeline upfront.

Who pulls the permits and deals with the building management?

We coordinate it. Permits are pulled by our licensed & insured Florida contractor partners; COI for your building, elevator bookings and noise-hour rules are part of our process, not your problem.

Can a closed condo kitchen be opened to the living room?

Sometimes. It depends on what the wall is: a simple partition can often be opened, while a structural or shared wall usually cannot. We check the building drawings and your association's rules before we design the new layout.
