Miami-Dade County

We work in Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, Brickell, Sunny Isles Beach, Aventura, Pinecrest, Coconut Grove, Key Biscayne and nearby.

What changes between a condo tower and a single-family house is mostly the process. In a tower, the building's rules shape the project: association approval, work hours, elevator bookings and a certificate of insurance. In a house, the structure, the roof and the openings come first. In Miami-Dade every opening needs approved impact protection. We plan for both. Read more about condo and apartment renovation and full home renovation.

02

Broward County

In Broward we work in Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Hallandale Beach and nearby areas.

The same team comes to you: site visits are by appointment, and samples come to your home. Broward is part of Florida's High-Velocity Hurricane Zone together with Miami-Dade, which shapes window and door choices. Each county, and many cities, run their own building department, so permit paths differ; our contractor partners pull the permits.

03

Palm Beach County

In Palm Beach County we work in Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Palm Beach and nearby areas.

Palm Beach County is the farthest area we serve from Miami, so we agree the visit time with you in advance, and it helps if you send photos and plans on WhatsApp before we arrive. The project then runs the same way as everywhere else, with weekly photo updates through handover.

Questions

Do you take projects outside Miami-Dade?

Yes. We work in Miami-Dade first, and in Broward and Palm Beach counties too.

Yes. Samples come to you: a master brings full-size panels to your home by appointment. See the sample library for the finishes.
