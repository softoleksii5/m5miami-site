What changes

What a kitchen renovation usually changes

Space from the next roomMany Miami kitchens are small and closed. Taking a partition out, or moving a pantry or wardrobe, can double the working area and let the kitchen join the living room. Only where the wall and the building allow it.
An island, or a better runA long island gives you prep space and seating. Where there is no room for one, a well-planned run under the window and a tall unit with a built-in oven do more than new fronts.
Furniture drawn for your roomCabinets made to drawings fit the walls, the pipes and the hood exactly. Light, sockets, ventilation and appliances are planned with them, not added at the end.

Typical Miami prices for 2026, based on market data and our own estimates. They help you plan; your own kitchen gets an itemized estimate 48 hours after a free site visit.

KitchenTypical price, Miami 2026
Refresh: new fronts, counters, backsplash, lighting$15–25K

High-end custom kitchen with island and joinery$100–200K

The cost follows how much of the kitchen moves. A new layout with relocated plumbing and ventilation costs more than the same footprint with new cabinets. The other drivers are cabinetry and joinery, stone counters and islands, appliances and lighting. Design can be bought on its own: typically $8–12 per sq ft for full interior design, more with custom joinery and furnishing. More numbers in our Miami renovation cost guide.

Payments are by milestone, with no large upfront payment. Changes happen only through a change order you sign first.

Process

How it works, in five steps

  1. Site visit (about a week). We measure, look at what is behind the walls and agree what you want.
  2. Concept (about 1–2 weeks). Two or three directions for layout and style.
  3. Design, 3D and budget (about 2–3 weeks). Layout, 3D, materials and the itemized budget.
  4. Approvals and construction. Building paperwork first, then the work per the estimate, with weekly photo updates and milestone payments.
  5. Handover. Final cleaning, a walkthrough, a punch-list and a 1-year written warranty.

FAQ

Kitchen renovation in Miami: questions

Do I need my condo association's approval for a kitchen renovation in Miami?

In most buildings, yes, once the work goes beyond cosmetic changes. A kitchen usually involves plumbing, electrical and ventilation, so check your building's rules and submit the plans before work starts. Every association has its own process. We prepare the paperwork and coordinate with the building; the exact requirements come from your association.

What do condo buildings usually require: COI, elevator and work hours?

Typically association approval for the plans, restricted work hours, a reserved service elevator and a certificate of insurance (COI) naming the association. Rules differ from building to building, so always check yours. In an M5 project, the COI for your building, elevator bookings and the plan for noise-hour rules are part of our process.

Do I need a permit for a kitchen remodel in Miami-Dade?

Generally yes when the work moves plumbing, changes electrical circuits or alters walls. A purely cosmetic refresh generally does not. Permits are pulled by our licensed & insured Florida contractor partners, who work under their own permit and contract. If you are unsure, ask your local building department.

Can you take down a wall to open the kitchen to the living room?

Sometimes. It depends on what the wall is: a simple partition can often be opened, while a structural or shared wall usually cannot. In a condo we check the building drawings and the association's rules before we design the new layout. In a house, we check the structure first.

How long does a kitchen renovation take?

The design stage takes roughly 4–6 weeks, from the first consultation to the design, 3D and itemized budget. On site, a refresh (new fronts, counters, backsplash) typically takes 2–4 weeks, a full kitchen remodel 6–10 weeks, and a custom kitchen with made-to-order joinery 10–14 weeks because of cabinet lead times. Condo approval and any permit come before the work starts and depend on the building and the city. You get the timeline in writing before you sign, with weekly photo updates once work starts.

Can you renovate my kitchen while I am away?

Yes, if you are comfortable approving things remotely. You approve the design and the itemized estimate, and sign any change before it is made. You get weekly photo updates through handover, with a final walkthrough and punch-list at the end.

Who supplies the appliances and makes the cabinets?

Cabinetry and built-in furniture are designed by us and made to drawings for your room, so they fit the walls, the plumbing and the ventilation. Appliances are chosen at the design stage and planned together with ventilation and lighting, so nothing is added as an afterthought.
