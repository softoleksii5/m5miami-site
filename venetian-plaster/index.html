How much does Venetian plaster cost in Miami?

Projects start at $10,000. The price depends on the finish, the wall area and the condition of the surface. We give a fixed written price after a free site visit.

Venetian plaster or Marmorino?

Venetian plaster is polished to a smooth, deep, marble-like shine. Marmorino has a finer stone texture and a softer, satin look. We bring both to your site visit.

Can it go in a bathroom?

Yes. Venetian plaster and Marmorino are common in bathrooms and powder rooms. Wet zones get a protective sealer.

How long does it take?

A feature wall or a powder room usually takes several days, including drying between coats. You get the schedule in writing with the price.

Do you work with designers and contractors?

Yes. We match the designer's sample and fit into the contractor's schedule. Trade pricing and sample panels for your studio.
