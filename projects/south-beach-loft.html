South Beach Loft

An industrial South Beach loft, fitted out to keep its raw character — exposed structure and concrete — while adding warmth with oak and linen.

The brief

Keep the loft's edge, lose the coldness. A creative, comfortable home that still reads industrial.

Our approach

The exposed ceiling was preserved and the concrete floor polished by our contractor partners; we then softened the shell with warm oak joinery, linen furniture and tall-window styling.

The result

A characterful, livable loft delivered in 10 weeks, fixed-scope, with weekly updates.

What a South Beach loft fit-out costs

A loft fit-out of this size — around 1,030 sq ft — typically starts near $150K, depending on how much of the raw shell you keep and your finish level. Every M5 project is fixed-scope and fully itemized before you sign.

Scope of work

  • Preserve & refinish exposed structure
  • Polished concrete flooring
  • Warm oak joinery
  • Lighting design for high ceilings
  • Full styling & fit-out
