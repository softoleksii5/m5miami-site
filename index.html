Why plaster

Not paint. Not tile. Stone made on the wall.

Mineral plaster is troweled by hand, coat over coat, and burnished until the wall reads like stone. No seams, no wallpaper joints, no grout lines. One master takes it from the first coat to sign-off.

01

Seamless

One continuous surface across walls, ceilings, columns and millwork. No joints, no grout, no visible panels.

02

Breathable

Mineral, not plastic. The surface lets moisture pass instead of trapping it under a film, which is what Miami humidity and air conditioning ask for.

03

Built for decades

Paint fades and peels. Plaster cures harder with time. Washable, UV-stable and made for Florida sun, indoors and on façades.

04

Repairable

A scuff or a chip is patched and blended by the same master. No repainting the whole wall.

FAQ

Venetian plaster in Miami: what people ask first.

Does Venetian plaster hold up in Miami humidity and air conditioning?

Yes. Mineral plaster is breathable: it lets moisture move through instead of trapping it under a film, which is what makes paint peel here. In wet zones we add a sealer. It handles AC cycles and the seasonal humidity swings.

Can it go in a bathroom or a shower?

Bathrooms and powder rooms, yes: Venetian and Marmorino are the usual choices there. Inside the shower we use a sealed system made for direct water. We confirm the right material during the site visit.

What drives the price?

Material and finish, total area, the condition of the substrate, ceilings and curved surfaces, and custom color. Projects start at $10,000. After a free measure you get a fixed written price.

How long does it take?

A sample takes 48 hours. An accent wall is usually two to three days on site; a full room about a week, including drying between coats. You get the schedule with the quote.

We live in a condo. What about the building rules?

We work inside condo hours, book the elevator, provide the certificate of insurance your management asks for and keep dust controlled. Tell us the building, we handle the rest.

How do I care for it, and can it be repaired?

Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap, no abrasives. A scuff or chip is patched and blended by our master so the repair disappears into the finish. No repainting the whole wall.
