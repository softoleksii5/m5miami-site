Condo owners

What your condo building will ask for before a renovation

Every building has its own rules. These are the things owners meet most often.

  • The building’s renovation rules and applicationAsk management for both before you design anything.
  • Plans approved by the associationFor work beyond cosmetic changes, the plans usually get reviewed before work starts.
  • The contractor’s insurance certificateMost buildings want it on file, naming the building, before work begins.
  • A licensed, insured contractorSome buildings require one, or keep a list of approved contractors.
  • Work hours and noise limitsNoisy work is allowed only on set days and hours, so the schedule is planned around them.
  • The service elevator, booked aheadMaterials, deliveries and debris move on reserved elevator times, with common areas protected.
  • A deposit for the common areasMany buildings hold one against damage. Terms vary, so ask.
We help with the paperwork. Your building’s own rules always come first.
