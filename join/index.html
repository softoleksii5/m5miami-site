04 / What we never do
Camera-roll grid. Unedited site photos + holiday graphics + a random meme. No grade, no rhythm — reads cheap.
Collage-and-watermark disease. Before/after crammed into one split image with arrows and a phone number. Kills the reveal.
Reels without hooks. A 60-second doorway pan with no text in the first 2 seconds. Viewers are gone by second 3.
Trophy-only feed. Glam finals in a burst, then months of silence. No process, no people — nothing to trust.
Caption = hashtag soup. 30 hashtags, zero sentences, "Call now!!". A $200K brand explains its decisions instead.
