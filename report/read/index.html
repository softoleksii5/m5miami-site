What not to do

10 mistakes that cost six figures to fix

✗A rug that fits under the coffee table and nothing else.
✓Size up: front legs of the sofa and the armchairs on the rug.
✗One overhead light per room.
✓Layer it: ambient, task, accent.
✗Small art on big walls.
✓Go bigger. Center of the canvas about 57 in (145 cm) from the floor.
✗Glossy tile anywhere near water. A slip hazard, and it reads cheap.
✓Matte, non-slip stone or tile.
✗Designing for the photo, not the morning.
✓Prep space, storage and traffic flow come first.
✗Chasing what's trending on social media.
✓Choose what you'll want in ten years.
✗Small windows on the side with the view, big ones facing the neighbor.
✓Big windows on the side with the view.
✗All glazing facing west. In Miami that is heat, glare and a power bill you'll notice.
✓Spread the glass and shade the west side with deep overhangs.
✗Open shelving in a humid kitchen. Dust, grease and salt air.
✓Closed cabinets.
✗Forgetting hurricane code until the inspection.
✓Approved impact protection on every opening, planned from day one.
Rule of thumb. If a decision touches the slab, the roof or an opening, it costs ten times more to change after framing than on paper. Decide those first.
What's over

8 things that are over

  • All-white interiors.The gallery look reads as unfinished now.
  • Grey on grey.Floors, walls, sofa, all grey. It's a rental.
  • Glossy everything.High-gloss kitchens and lacquered walls show fingerprints and date instantly.
  • Beige-on-beige "quiet luxury."The idea was good; the execution turned into oatmeal.
  • Pale Scandinavian oak.Yellows in Florida sun within two years.
  • Visible smart-home gadgets.Keypads, exposed speakers, a tablet on the wall.
  • Matching furniture sets.Sofa, loveseat, armchair, same fabric. Nobody has lived in this room.
  • Dark mirror pools.Looks like black resin from the terrace and hides what's at the bottom.
What replaced it

10 trends worth your money

  1. Warm earth palettesClay, sand, terracotta, olive. Cool white goes sterile by noon in Miami light.
  2. Textured wallsVenetian plaster, stone, handmade tile. One hero wall per room, never an accent patch.
  3. Medium and warm woodWalnut, smoked oak, teak tones. Match it across cabinetry, doors and floors.
  4. Natural stone as architectureLarge slabs on fireplaces, stairs and bath walls. Look at the slab, not the sample.
  5. Curves and rounded formsArched openings, rounded islands, plaster niches. Curves belong in plaster.
  6. Garden roomsA covered room that belongs to both the house and the garden. Plan overhangs and drainage early.
  7. Wellness at homeSauna, cold plunge, steam shower. Ventilation and drainage from day one.
  8. Analog roomsA library, a listening room, a card room. No screens, good light, heavy curtains.
  9. Hidden technologyScreens behind joinery, speakers in the ceiling. Run conduit during framing.
  10. Resilience built inImpact glass, raised floors, deep overhangs, materials you can repair.
